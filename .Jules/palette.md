## 2026-07-20 - Screen Reader Announcements for Clipboard Copy Interactions
**Learning:** Client-side copy-to-clipboard interactions lack standard visual-to-auditory parity because changing the button text (e.g., from "Copy" to "Copied") is not automatically announced by screen readers. By introducing a visually hidden, shared `aria-live="polite"` element and dynamically updating both the element text and caching/restoring the original `aria-label` attribute on the copy button itself, we ensure robust, polite, and accessible announcements without risking rapid successive click state corruption.
**Action:** Always maintain a visually hidden singleton `<div aria-live="polite" class="sr-only">` helper on the document body for any quick client-side interactions (like copying to clipboard or toggling settings), and update it with contextual text during events. Always cache original interaction attributes (`aria-label`, `title`) in variables during hook mounting to guarantee clean restoration on timeouts or unmounting.
