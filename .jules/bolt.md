## 2026-07-19 - Case-Insensitive Plan Matching Optimization
**Learning:** In Elixir, runtime case-insensitive regular expression matching (e.g., `plan =~ ~r/enterprise|team/i`) compiles the regular expression and runs complex match engines on every invocation, causing significant overhead in hot paths. Replacing this with a combination of `String.downcase/1` and `String.contains?/2` yields more than a 1.8x speedup. Furthermore, passing a list of substrings to `String.contains?/2` is substantially slower than using explicit `or` conditions because list matching compiles the search patterns dynamically at runtime, whereas separate sequential `contains?` calls compile down to static binary matches.
**Action:** Always favor `String.downcase/1` and sequential `String.contains?/2` with explicit `or` conditions over `=~ ~r/.../i` or `String.contains?(downcase, [...list...])` in latency-sensitive execution flows.
